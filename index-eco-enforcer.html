12 / SDGREPAIR · REUSE · RECYCLE

01 / Understand

The problem we see.

SDG 12 promotes more sustainable consumption and production. Our focus is the electronic devices we use every day.

Electronic waste is growing.

Phones, laptops and accessories are sometimes replaced while they can still be repaired or reused. When electronics reach the end of their useful life, responsible collection and recycling help recover materials and reduce harm.

Explore the UN goal ↗
↺

Think before you replace.

Can you repair it? Could someone else use it? If neither is possible, take it to an appropriate e-waste collection point.

Clean streets. Tough fights.

Move with WASD or arrows. Hold Space to fire energy pulses at the nearest enemy. Dash with Shift, collect rubbish and keep pollution below 100%.

Eliminate the cartoon opponents and collect dropped litter before pollution overwhelms the park.

WASD / arrows: move · Space or hold ATTACK: fire · Shift: dash · P: pause. Walk over rubbish and health kits to collect them. Your energy blaster automatically targets the nearest opponent.

Fictional arcade combat. In real life, avoid confrontation and report unsafe littering to the appropriate authorities.

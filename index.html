01 / UNDERSTAND

The problem we see.

Electronic waste is growing.

Phones, laptops and other devices are often replaced before the end of their useful life. Repairing and reusing electronics can help reduce waste and save resources. Responsible recycling helps keep valuable materials in circulation.
